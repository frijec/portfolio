About · How I work

01

Constraints first. I write down what makes the problem hard before I open a design tool, and I show that list to the team.

02

Every decision has a rejected alternative and a cost. If I can't name both, it isn't a decision yet.

03

Systems over screens. Tokens, components and governance ship with the product — at Golisto I own that as CPO, at Consid I build it for clients.

04

Two hats, kept honest. Consultant at Consid Danmark, founder at Golisto and Tagga. The founder work is where I test what I recommend to clients.
