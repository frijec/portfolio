02 · Situation

Norlys is Denmark's largest integrated energy and telecoms cooperative, serving over 1.7 million customers across electricity, broadband, mobile, TV and EV charging. Skærm Tiden is its screen-time app: limits on specific apps, weekly usage reports that hold this week up against the last, and restrictions that follow both time and location, so work apps can be throttled at home, or the reverse.

A team of Consid consultants built it, working with Norlys' internal marketing department and an external marketing and communications partner.
