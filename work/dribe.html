02 · Situation

:Dribe was a Danish car subscription service: flexible, app-based access to a car with no long-term commitment. The brief was repositioning: establish it as a car concept that surpassed any leasing platform, built on a plug-and-play service that adapted to what an individual needed from a car.

That meant a complete redesign of every digital platform and a thorough overhaul of the physical touchpoints in the service design, rather than one screen at a time. Alongside the redesign I helped :Dribe assemble a Customer Journey team, put together so its members covered each other's strengths and weaknesses, to carry the platform after the project ended.
