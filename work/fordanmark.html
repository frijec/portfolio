02 · Situation

Dansk Boldspil-Union is the governing body for football in Denmark. The work was ForDanmark.dk, the site for the national teams: a simple, modular presentation of exclusive content across teams, matches and tournaments: articles, video, images and statistics gathered in one place, built around material you cannot get anywhere else.

Every content module was designed to sit correctly in any context, developed out of an existing visual identity and carried into a digital format. I ran every part of it, from the opening creative workshops and the ideation of the experience concept through to the key screens and the visual direction.
