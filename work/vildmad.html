Vild Mad

A foraging guide rebuilt around geodata: what is edible near you, and when it is in season.

Spec
RoleUX / Design Lead
ClientVild Mad
PlatformsiOS / Android
Duration2020 — 2021
02 · Situation

Vild Mad is a Danish initiative that encourages people to forage and cook with the wild plants, mushrooms and seaweed found in Denmark's landscape. My team carried out a complete overhaul of the app and rebuilt it around its resources.

To improve the experience we combined geodata with what users reported, so the app could say what was in season and verified nearby. Someone heading out had a real chance of coming back with something.
